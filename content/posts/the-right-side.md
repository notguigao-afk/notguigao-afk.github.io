Edwin Lefèvre 在《[Reminiscences of a Stock Operator](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/60979/60979-h/60979-h.htm)》第三章中写道：

> There is only one side to the stock market; and it is not the bull side or the bear side, but the right side.
